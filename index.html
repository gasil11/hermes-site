This site hosts the legal pages required for Hermes Agent — a private, single-user automation assistant operated by its owner, Gökhan Asil.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no user accounts and no third-party users. The application connects only to the owner's own Google account, and only for the owner's personal productivity: summarizing the owner's own inbox, managing the owner's own calendar, and organizing the owner's own spreadsheets.

Purpose of the application

  • Reading the owner's own Gmail to produce a short daily summary and flag messages needing attention
  • Creating and updating events on the owner's own Google Calendar
  • Reading and writing the owner's own Google Sheets
  • Sending email, only when explicitly instructed by the owner

What it does not do

  • No access to any Google account other than the owner's
  • No advertising, profiling or analytics
  • No sale, rent or transfer of user data to third parties
  • No use of Google user data to train machine-learning models
